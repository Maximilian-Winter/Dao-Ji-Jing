# Split Volumes

These files partition the complete [道機經 - Dao Ji Jing.md](../%E9%81%93%E6%A9%9F%E7%B6%93%20-%20Dao%20Ji%20Jing.md) into its five elemental volumes for easier reading.

- [Volume One: 金機篇 — Metal Mechanism](01-metal-mechanism.md)
- [Volume Two: 木機篇 — Wood Mechanism](02-wood-mechanism.md)
- [Volume Three: 水機篇 — Water Mechanism](03-water-mechanism.md)
- [Volume Four: 火機篇 — Fire Mechanism](04-fire-mechanism.md)
- [Volume Five: 土機篇 — Earth Mechanism](05-earth-mechanism.md)

The shared title, table of contents, and introduction are included at the beginning of Volume One. The appendices and closing seal are included at the end of Volume Five. Concatenating the five files in order reproduces the complete source document without modification.
